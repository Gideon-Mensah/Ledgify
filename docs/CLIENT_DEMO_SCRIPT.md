# Ledgify Client Demo Script

Target duration: 20 minutes. Use Ledgify Demo Ltd; the former £375 AR reporting blocker is resolved and accounting health passes.

1. **Login and organisation (1 minute).** Sign in, select Ledgify Demo Ltd, and show that the organisation selector controls the active company.
2. **Dashboard (1 minute).** Explain live cash, receivable, payable, profit, inventory, and recent-activity information.
3. **Customers (1 minute).** Open Bluewave Consulting Ltd and show its contact record and statement.
4. **Invoices (2 minutes).** Open DEMO-INV-002 as the fallback partial invoice. Show lines, journal link, paid amount, and amount outstanding. Explain draft editing versus posted immutability.
5. **Customer payment (1 minute).** Demonstrate the payment dialog without posting if the demo database should remain unchanged. Explain partial and final allocation.
6. **Suppliers and bills (2 minutes).** Open Amazon Business and DEMO-BILL-002. Show bill details, payment history, amount due, and journal link.
7. **Banking (2 minutes).** Show the Business Current Account, linked ledger, book/statement balances, unreconciled transactions, import preview, and reconciliation suggestions.
8. **Reconciliation (1 minute).** Explain that existing payments are matched rather than recreated. Show cash coding using only non-control accounts.
9. **Chart of Accounts (1 minute).** Search an account, open Account Details, and show posted activity.
10. **Manual journal (1 minute).** Show the balanced-entry form, account restrictions, and period-lock protection. Do not post unless the demo script calls for it.
11. **Reports (3 minutes).** Open Trial Balance, drill into an account, then show P&L and Balance Sheet with preserved report dates and exports.
12. **Inventory (1 minute).** Show products, warehouse stock, WAC valuation, Stock Movements, and the Stock Adjustments audit trail.
13. **Fixed Assets and Manufacturing (1 minute).** Show the available register/BOM/order screens. The current seed does not guarantee fallback asset or production-order records.
14. **Financial Analysis (1 minute).** Show backend-authoritative ratios, methodology, comparison, exports, and Ask AI.
15. **AI and Settings (1 minute).** Explain read-only context, controlled proposals, permissions, organisation settings, and documented limitations.

Fallback records: DEMO-INV-001 paid, DEMO-INV-002 partial, DEMO-INV-003 open; DEMO-BILL-001 paid, DEMO-BILL-002 partial, DEMO-BILL-003 open; DEMO-BANK-1/2 unreconciled; products LAPTOP, MONITOR, CHAIR, PAPER, SUPPORT.
